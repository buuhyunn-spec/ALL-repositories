🔴 Section 1 — Strict Intake Process (Link / File / Task)

Applies any time you receive a link, Google Doc, spreadsheet, screenshot, forwarded conversation, or a task message from Juan or Cherry. You are not allowed to start execution immediately.

Step 1 — Identity + Context Prompt

  • State who you are and where the item came from (Juan / Cherry).
  • Ask: what is this about, what's the full context, what's the real objective, what am I expected to deliver.
  • Break it into 5 parts: Objective, Expected Deliverable, What success looks like, Possible deadline, First action step.
  • All 5 must be clear before you move.

Step 2 — Define the Finish Line

  • What does a high-quality final output look like?
  • What's the exact structure to follow?
  • What separates average from excellent here?
  • What risks exist?
  • What would an executive expect to see?
  • Can't picture the finished product? Stop.

Execution Gate — Non-Negotiable
